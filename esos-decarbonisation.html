Decarbonisation Operating System

From climate ambition to enterprise transformation. An integrated, end-to-end framework for credible, measurable, and science-aligned emissions reduction across the full value chain.

5 layers 13 steps GHG Protocol · SBTi · ISO 14064 · CSRD
Overview Framework Operating Model
Overview

Achieving net zero requires more than setting targets. It requires a coordinated operating model - one that connects governance, emissions accounting, transition planning, technology, supplier engagement, capital allocation, and verified reporting into a single system.

This framework is built on one principle: deep emissions reduction must come before addressing residuals. It draws on the GHG Protocol, ISO greenhouse gas standards, and the SBTi Net-Zero Standard to take organisations from measurement into implementation.

Most emissions sit outside direct operations - in suppliers, materials, logistics, product use, and end-of-life systems. Decarbonisation therefore demands coordinated action across the full value chain, not just within the fence line.

Understand → Prioritise → Transform → Embed → Verify → Improve

This model enables organisations to treat decarbonisation not as a standalone sustainability initiative, but as an enterprise transformation agenda.

Core principle
Decarbonisation is not a carbon reduction project. It is an enterprise transition system - one that must be governed, resourced, and measured like any other strategic transformation.
