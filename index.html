About Me
ESG Intelligence · Strategic Foresight · Decision Support

An independent, curated ESG Knowledge Hub. Born out of passion and frustration.

We, the sustainability professionals deal with a broad range of complexities everyday:

  • Evolving Regulations,
  • Geo-political Uncertainties,
  • Mismatched Business priorities, and more.

Ours is a constant battle - to Comprehend, to Construct, to Communicate, to Convince, and to Comply.

And using AI for Sustainability has become a curse, and a boon!
(And no, this text is all my own words)

My intention here is to collate a repository of know-how categorised into specific audience types - for the leadership, practitioners, in-house ESG teams and the curious minded.

Allowing you, my fellow professionals to do what you need to do - ACT.
