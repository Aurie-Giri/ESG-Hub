Scoring guide
Likelihood
1 — Rare (unlikely within horizon)
2 — Unlikely
3 — Possible
4 — Likely
5 — Almost certain
Impact
1 — Negligible (minimal effect)
2 — Minor
3 — Moderate
4 — Major
5 — Severe (threatens continuity)
Score = Likelihood × Impact. 1–6 Low · 7–14 Medium · 15–25 High (material — carried into Response Planning)
