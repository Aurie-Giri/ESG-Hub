Tools & Templates · Materiality Decision Tool

Materiality - What actually matters.

A three-stage decision tool aligned to the post-Omnibus ESRS (C(2026) 5010). Identifies material topics, ranks them by impact and financial significance, and generates board-ready narrative - in CFO and CEO language, not sustainability language.

Structured around the top-down materiality approach in paragraph 27 of the revised ESRS 1 (July 2026). Not yet in force pending Official Journal publication.

Answer six questions about your business. The tool will apply the top-down materiality logic from ESRS 1 paragraph 27 and pre-filter the eleven ESRS topics - flagging which are likely material before you score a single datapoint.

Primary sector
Choose the sector that best describes your main business activity
Primary markets and geographies
Where do you primarily operate or sell?
Value chain intensity
How complex is your upstream supply chain?
Workforce profile
What describes your employment footprint?
Primary product or service type
What do you mainly produce or deliver?
Reporting wave
Which CSRD reporting cohort applies to you?
Topic pre-filter - based on your answers

Topics marked out have low probability of materiality given your sector, geography, and business model. You may conclude these immaterial at topic level under ESRS 1 paragraph 27 - record your reasoning. You can override any topic by clicking it.

Phasing reliefs applicable to your wave

Score each topic on two dimensions. Impact materiality - how significant is your company's effect on this topic? Financial materiality - how significant is this topic's effect on your business value? The live map updates as you score. Topics concluded out in Stage 1 are pre-set to zero - you can still score them if you wish.
