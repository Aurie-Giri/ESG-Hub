ISO 14001:2015
Environmental Management
What
Environmental management system requirements; certifiable standard.
Why
Helps control environmental risks, support compliance, and create structured ESG data.
When
Use when environmental impacts, legal obligations, or site-level controls matter.
How
Define scope, identify environmental aspects, set objectives, implement controls, monitor performance, review improvement.
Leadership adviceTreat environmental performance as a managed operating system, not a reporting task.
Next step →Run a gap assessment against current environmental controls and evidence.
ISO 50001:2018
Energy Management
What
Energy management system requirements; certifiable standard.
Why
Reduces energy cost, improves efficiency, and supports decarbonization evidence.
When
Use when energy is a material cost or emissions driver.
How
Establish energy baseline, define EnPIs, identify significant energy uses, implement action plans, track and improve.
Leadership adviceEnergy efficiency should be managed like a business performance metric.
Next step →Identify the top three energy-consuming processes or sites.
ISO 14064-1:2018
Organizational GHG Accounting
What
Requirements and guidance for organizational GHG quantification; can be used as a verifiable inventory framework.
Why
Supports CSRD climate data, CBAM-related emissions control, and audit-ready carbon reporting.
When
Use when the company reports Scope 1, 2, and relevant Scope 3 emissions.
How
Set organizational boundaries, collect emissions data, apply calculation methods, document assumptions, review quality.
Leadership adviceClimate data is only useful if it is consistent, comparable, and auditable.
Next step →Map all emissions sources to owners and data systems.
ISO 14067:2018
Product Carbon Footprint
What
Product carbon footprint requirements; not a management system, but used for product-level carbon accounting.
Why
Strongly relevant for DPP, CBAM-related product data, and customer carbon disclosure.
When
Use for products with carbon claims, supplier data needs, or eco-design work.
How
Define product system boundary, collect life-cycle data, calculate footprint, document methods, verify if needed.
Leadership adviceProduct carbon numbers must be traceable to source data, not estimates alone.
Next step →Select one priority product and pilot a footprint calculation.
ISO 20400:2017
Sustainable Procurement
What
Guidance standard for sustainable procurement; not certifiable.
Why
Supports CSDDD, EUDR, CSRD supply chain disclosures, and supplier risk control.
When
Use when procurement influences ESG outcomes or supplier compliance.
How
Embed sustainability criteria in sourcing, supplier selection, contracts, due diligence, monitoring, and improvement.
Leadership adviceProcurement is one of the fastest ways to turn ESG policy into real operational change.
Next step →Add sustainability clauses to the highest-risk supplier category.
ISO 37301:2021
Compliance Management
What
Compliance management system requirements and guidance; certifiable standard.
Why
Helps structure legal compliance, accountability, monitoring, and escalation.
When
Use where multiple regulations, controls, and responsibilities need coordination.
How
Identify obligations, assign ownership, train people, monitor compliance, escalate breaches, review effectiveness.
Leadership adviceCompliance should be designed into the system, not checked only after something goes wrong.
Next step →Build a legal and ESG obligations register.
ISO 27001:2022
Information Security
What
Information security management system requirements; certifiable standard.
Why
Supports data integrity for ESG reporting, AI governance, and control over sensitive supplier or workforce data.
When
Use when data quality, cyber risk, or sensitive information is material.
How
Define ISMS scope, assess risks, implement controls, monitor incidents, review continually.
Leadership adviceESG reporting is only as trustworthy as the underlying information controls.
Next step →Identify the systems that feed CSRD or compliance reporting.
ISO 31000:2018
Risk Management
What
Risk management guidelines; guidance standard, not certifiable.
Why
Supports enterprise risk alignment, climate risk, compliance risk, and operational resilience.
When
Use when sustainability risks need to be integrated into governance and planning.
How
Establish context, assess risks, treat risks, monitor, communicate, and review.
Leadership adviceSustainability risk should be treated as business risk.
Next step →Add ESG risks to the enterprise risk register.
ISO 45001:2018
Occupational Health & Safety
What
Occupational health and safety management system requirements; certifiable standard.
Why
Supports worker protection, due diligence, and stronger social performance.
When
Use where operational safety, contractor safety, or labor conditions are material.
How
Identify hazards, assess risks, implement controls, train, investigate incidents, improve.
Leadership adviceA safe workplace is a governance issue, not only an HR issue.
Next step →Review the top incident causes and high-risk work activities.
ISO 14006:2020
Ecodesign
What
Guidelines for incorporating ecodesign into an EMS; guidance standard, not certifiable.
Why
Relevant for ESPR, DPP, PPWR, EPR, and product redesign for lower impact.
When
Use when product design choices affect material use, repairability, recyclability, or footprint.
How
Link design teams to environmental criteria, define product requirements, evaluate impacts early, iterate design.
Leadership adviceThe biggest sustainability gains often happen before production starts.
Next step →Add ecodesign criteria to one product development process.

Supporting Compliance Standards

ISO 14004:2016
EMS Implementation Guidance
What
EMS implementation guidance; guidance standard.
Why
Helps build a practical EMS faster, with fewer design mistakes.
When
Use when ISO 14001 is being introduced or strengthened.
How
Use it to set structure, roles, documentation, objectives, and operational controls.
Leadership adviceA good EMS design makes compliance easier to sustain.
Next step →Use it as the playbook for your ISO 14001 rollout.
ISO 14005:2019
Phased EMS Implementation
What
Phased implementation guidance for EMS; guidance standard.
Why
Useful where maturity is low or rollout must be gradual.
When
Use in multi-site or resource-constrained organizations.
How
Implement stepwise, prioritize material aspects, and add controls over time.
Leadership adviceA phased approach is better than a perfect plan that never starts.
Next step →Define a phase 1 EMS scope and timeline.
ISO 14008:2019
Monetary Valuation of Environmental Impacts
What
Monetary valuation of environmental impacts; guidance standard.
Why
Helps translate environmental effects into business language for investment decisions.
When
Use for business cases, capex prioritization, or impact comparisons.
How
Apply valuation methods to selected impacts and use results in decision-making.
Leadership adviceCosts and impacts become easier to act on when they are expressed in the same language.
Next step →Test it on one investment or product redesign case.
ISO 14020 / 14021 / 14024 / 14025
Environmental Labels & Declarations
What
Environmental labels, self-claims, eco-labels, and EPDs; mostly guidance frameworks, with different claim structures.
Why
Supports truthful product communication, DPP evidence, PPWR packaging claims, and reduced greenwashing risk.
When
Use when making environmental claims about products or packaging.
How
Match the claim type to the right standard, verify data, avoid unsupported statements, document methods.
Leadership adviceEnvironmental claims must be evidence-based or they become a liability.
Next step →Review all current product claims against evidence.
ISO 14031:2013
Environmental Performance Evaluation
What
Environmental performance evaluation guidance; guidance standard.
Why
Helps define KPIs and track environmental improvement over time.
When
Use when environmental performance needs to be measured and managed.
How
Select indicators, collect data, analyze trends, and feed results into management review.
Leadership adviceWhat gets measured gets managed.
Next step →Define a small set of environmental KPIs.
ISO 14040 / 14044
Life Cycle Assessment
What
Life cycle assessment principles and requirements; guidance framework with detailed requirements.
Why
Core for ESPR, DPP, PPWR, EPR, product redesign, and footprint evidence.
When
Use when product impacts must be compared across the life cycle.
How
Define goal and scope, inventory data, impact assessment, interpretation, review.
Leadership adviceProduct sustainability decisions should be based on life-cycle evidence, not isolated assumptions.
Next step →Pick one product and map its life-cycle data needs.
ISO 14046:2014
Water Footprint
What
Water footprint standard; guidance framework.
Why
Useful where water stress, water disclosure, or site-specific water risks matter.
When
Use in water-intensive operations or regions with water scarcity.
How
Define water use boundaries, gather data, assess impacts, report results.
Leadership adviceWater risk should be managed before it becomes operational disruption.
Next step →Identify the highest-water-use site or process.
ISO 14064-2 / 14064-3 / 14065 / 14066
GHG Verification & Validation
What
Project GHG quantification, validation, verification, and verifier competence; standards used for credible carbon claims.
Why
Supports CBAM, carbon projects, and assurance of climate data.
When
Use when emission reduction projects or third-party verification are needed.
How
Define project boundaries, calculate reductions, verify independently, ensure competent reviewers.
Leadership adviceVerified climate data is more valuable than unverified ambition.
Next step →Decide which GHG data needs third-party assurance.
