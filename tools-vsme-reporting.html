VSME Reporting Tool - Voluntary Standard

A structured offline tool for undertakings using the Voluntary Sustainability Reporting Standard - Commission Delegated Regulation C(2026) 5011, adopted 3 July 2026. Complete your Basic and/or Comprehensive Module disclosures, generate a shadow sustainability report, and understand the value chain cap. No data leaves your device.

Organisation Size

Employee size

This determines which datapoints are essential vs voluntary for your organisation, and defines your position under the value chain cap.

10 or fewer employees
Micro-undertaking. Several environmental and energy datapoints (B3, B6, B7, C3, C4, C5, C6, C7) become voluntary rather than essential. Lower value chain cap applies.
More than 10 employees
Standard VSME entity. Full Basic Module applies as essential. Comprehensive Module essential items apply where relevant. Higher value chain cap applies.

Legal status: C(2026) 5011 is adopted but not yet published in the Official Journal. It enters into force three days after publication. The VSME Recommendation (EU) 2025/1710 of 22 July 2025 is superseded on entry into force of this regulation. No assurance is required under the voluntary standard.
Value chain cap: Mandatory CSRD reporters (>€450m turnover and >1,000 employees) are legally prohibited from requesting more sustainability data from your organisation than what is covered by this voluntary standard. You have a statutory right to refuse requests that go beyond the cap. The Value Chain Cap tab shows exactly which items are within the cap.

Action items

    Disclosure Responses

    Disclosures

    Complete each datapoint that applies to your organisation. Essential datapoints should be completed. If applicable datapoints should be completed where the condition is met. Voluntary datapoints (for ≤10 employee organisations) are optional. Mark any genuinely non-applicable items as N/A - do not leave them blank.
    Value Chain Cap - Annex II to C(2026) 5011

    Value Chain Cap

    What this means: Mandatory CSRD reporters with more than €450m turnover and more than 1,000 employees are legally prohibited from requesting more sustainability information from your organisation than what is listed in Annex II to C(2026) 5011. You have a statutory right to refuse any request that goes beyond this cap. Where a request does go beyond the cap, the mandatory reporter must inform you in writing that it is beyond the cap and that you may decline.
    Important: Several items in the cap - including employee headcount by contract type, accident rate, minimum wage compliance, collective bargaining coverage, and training hours - apply to both ≤10 and >10 employee organisations. Energy, GHG, water, and waste data can only be requested from >10 employee organisations.
    Shadow Sustainability Report

    Shadow Sustainability Report

    This is a structured preview of your sustainability statement based on your completed disclosures. It is not a legally binding document - it is a working draft for internal review, board sign-off, or supply chain communication. Blank fields are flagged.
    Audit Trail

    Audit Trail

    A complete record of every datapoint - its status, your response, and the evidence source cited. Suitable for internal review or supply chain documentation purposes.
