Near-Term Targets

SBTi Near-Term Target Setting

SBTi near-term targets (5–10 years) use the Absolute Contraction Approach (ACA) - a linear annual reduction applied to the base-year total - or the Sectoral Decarbonization Approach (SDA), an intensity-based method requiring an output metric and a sector convergence benchmark. Near-term targets have different validation and offset-use rules than the long-term net-zero target modelled in the next tab - keep them visually and analytically separate.

Long-Term Target

SBTi Net-Zero Standard - Long-Term Trajectory

Long-term target: at least 90% absolute reduction of Scope 1+2+3 vs. base year, by no later than 2050. Residual emissions remaining at the net-zero year must be matched 1:1 with permanent carbon removals with durable storage - not interim offsets or beyond-value-chain mitigation (BVCM), which do not count toward the target itself.
